# Glossary

- **Status:** DRAFT. No definition is approved yet.
- **Last updated:** 2026-11-24
- **Owner:** business-analyst · **Task:** TASK-006 (Phase 2 — P03 Business Analysis)

Business terms, defined once and used consistently across requirements, architecture, database, backend, and frontend. Requirements are in [REQUIREMENTS.md](../requirements/REQUIREMENTS.md), business rules in [business-rules-catalog.md](business-rules-catalog.md), and open points link to [open-questions.md](../requirements/open-questions.md).

| Term | Definition (DRAFT) | Classification & Source | Open points |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Available stock** | The quantity of a product at a specific branch that can currently be allocated to a new order, transfer, or deduction. Must never become negative (`>= 0`). | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-002`, `REQ-007` / `BRULE-007`) | Reserved vs. available split: `B13` |
| **Branch** | One physical flower shop location owned by the business (initial target: 5–30 branches). Holds its own inventory, has its own product prices, and processes orders assigned to it. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-001`, `REQ-002`, `REQ-004`, `REQ-005`) | Delivery zone/coordinates format: `B3e` |
| **Branch-level authorization** | The security rule that every read or write on branch-owned data (stock, branch prices, branch orders, transfers) is checked on the server against the authenticated staff user's assigned branches. Client-supplied `branchId` is never trusted alone. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-011`, `REQ-012` / `BRULE-011`, `BRULE-012`) | Role permission matrix: `B5` |
| **Branch price** | The selling price of a catalog product at one specific branch. Prices may vary from branch to branch. | **CONFIRMED** (`B9`, `REQ-004` / `BRULE-004`) | Who sets it & missing price fallback: `B9b`. Which branch price is shown on storefront: `B11` |
| **Central product catalog** | The chain-wide master list of floral products, categories, and SKUs managed centrally by default. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-003` / `BRULE-003`) | Branch-specific products/hiding products: `B10`. Variants/seasonal rules: `B18` |
| **Central warehouse** | A non-store central stock facility. **Explicitly excluded in FlowerChain**: all inventory is held directly by branches. | **CONFIRMED** (`B2`, `REQ-016` / `BRULE-016`) | – |
| **Delivery order** | An online order placed on the storefront to be delivered to a customer-specified delivery address. | **CONFIRMED** (`B3a`, `B3b`, `REQ-013`, `REQ-014`) | Who delivers, delivery fee, date/time slots: `B15` |
| **Goods receipt (stock-in)** | Recording newly arrived stock at a branch (e.g., from a flower supplier), increasing the branch's available stock and writing an inventory audit record. | **INFERRED** (`REQ-002`, `REQ-008`, `REQ-016`) | Full supplier PO vs. simple stock-in: `B19` |
| **Head Office** | The central management team / business owner overseeing all branches, the central product catalog, staff roles, and chain-wide reporting. | **CONFIRMED** (`CLAUDE.md` §1, `REQ-001`, `REQ-003`) | Exact Head Office roles: `B5` |
| **Idempotent payment processing** | The guarantee that receiving the same payment notification or webhook event multiple times processes the payment at most once, with no duplicate state or inventory side effects. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-010` / `BRULE-010`) | Supported payment providers: `B4` |
| **In-transit stock** | Goods that have left a sending branch during an inter-branch stock transfer (`REQ-015`) and have not yet been confirmed as received by the receiving branch. | **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** (`B12`) | Single-step transfer vs. tracked in-transit state: `B12`, `B16` |
| **Inventory audit record** | An immutable log entry created for every change to a branch's stock, recording who made the change, when (UTC), why (reason/type), which branch, the quantity delta (`+N` or `-N`), and the source document ID. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-008` / `BRULE-008`, `CLAUDE.md` §6) | – |
| **Order allocation** | Determining and assigning the `processingBranchId` for an order. For home delivery orders, the system automatically selects the nearest branch with sufficient stock. | **CONFIRMED** (`B3b`, `REQ-005`, `REQ-014`) | Pickup allocation: `B3c`. Multi-branch shortfall: `B3d`. Distance metric: `B3e`. Reassignment: `B14` |
| **Order snapshot** | An immutable copy of product name, SKU, and unit price copied onto order lines when an order is placed so later catalog or branch price edits never alter historical orders. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-006` / `BRULE-006`, `CLAUDE.md` §6) | Which branch's price is snapshotted when auto-selected branch differs from browsing branch: `B11` |
| **Payment webhook** | A server-to-server HTTP callback from a payment provider notifying FlowerChain of a payment outcome. Must be cryptographically verified on the server before processing. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-009` / `BRULE-009`, `CLAUDE.md` §8) | Payment providers: `B4` |
| **Pickup order** | An online order placed on the storefront that the customer collects in person at a branch store. | **CONFIRMED** (`B3a`, `REQ-013`) | Who chooses the pickup store & whether it is always the processing branch: `B3c`. Pickup slots: `B15` |
| **Processing branch** | The single branch responsible for preparing and fulfilling an order (`branch_id NOT NULL`). Every order has a traceable processing branch. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-005` / `BRULE-005`, `B3b`) | Pickup orders: `B3c`. Split orders across branches: `B3d`. Moving an order: `B14` |
| **Reserved stock** | Stock temporarily held at a processing branch for an in-progress order prior to final deduction or release on cancellation/payment timeout. | **NEEDS CONFIRMATION** (`B13`) | Exact reservation timing and timeout rules: `B13` |
| **Spoilage / stock adjustment** | An audited deduction (or stocktake correction) of a branch's inventory when fresh flowers wilt, are damaged, or differ from physical counts. Must never reduce available stock below zero. | **INFERRED** (`REQ-002`, `REQ-007`, `REQ-008`) | Reason codes & approval thresholds: `B19` |
| **Stock (inventory)** | The quantity of each product held by a specific branch. There is no central warehouse; stock always belongs to one branch, never goes below zero, and every change is auditable. | **CONFIRMED** (`REQ-002`, `REQ-007`, `REQ-008`, `REQ-016`, `B2`) | Finished bouquets vs. raw stems: `B18` |
| **Stock transfer** | Moving goods from one branch's inventory to another branch's inventory. Every transfer is recorded and generates paired inventory audit records (`-N` at sender, `+N` at receiver). | **CONFIRMED** (`B2`, `REQ-015` / `BRULE-015`) | Requester/approver/receiver workflow: `B12`. Shortfalls, transit damage, cancellation: `B16` |
| **Storefront** | The customer-facing web application (`frontend/storefront`) used to browse products and branch prices, place delivery or pickup orders, and track order status. | **CONFIRMED** (`CLAUDE.md` §1, `DEC-005`) | Browsing branch selection: `B11`. Guest vs. account checkout: `B6` |
