# Business analysis

- **Owner:** business-analyst · **Phase:** 2 · **Status:** empty. Nothing has been analysed yet.

This folder holds descriptions of how the business works today and should work in future:

- `processes/`: one file per business process (e.g. order fulfilment, stock receiving), with steps, actors and exceptions.
- `actors-and-roles.md`: customer, staff and head-office roles and what each needs.
- `glossary.md`: business terms, defined once and used everywhere.

Every statement shows its source (owner interview, existing document) and is labelled CONFIRMED, INFERRED or NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Requirements that come out of the analysis go to [../requirements/REQUIREMENTS.md](../requirements/REQUIREMENTS.md).
